The call — where it lives

The hangar is the fourth rung of the zoom ladder, above the plot: HANGAR ↔ PLOT ↔ RAIL ↔ NODE, hard cuts, no tween — the ladder stays a contract, not a camera. Plot and below are per-room; the hangar is the only surface where all rooms exist at once. It is not a new window and not a mode: same console shell, one cut up.

The call — bays, not a starmap

Spatial position carries no meaning for rooms — a starmap invents distance where none exists (§2.6 in spirit: don't render invented data). Berth numbers are stable addresses (⌘1–⌘4 switch, ⌘0 for the hangar), the floor bus on the left is the one-global-audio-floor rule made physical, and the traffic strip IS the shared salience queue with room attribution — cross-room urgency reads without opening a single room. The starmap callout on the plot retires.

The call — one-off rooms look mortal

A scratch berth is dashed, spineless, and un-numbered. No plan glyph, no gear tag, no standing cast — Mikey narrates it. When its craft settles, the berth dissolves; nothing bolts on. Full-ceremony berths show their spine as a glyph (live / queued / settled blocks) because the spine is the room's mind and the berth is its face.

The commission writes a file — nothing else. The daemon watches the manifests directory the same way it watches queue files today; a new manifest = a new berth on the next snapshot. The call — a catalog, not a storybook

No knob panels, no prop playgrounds. Each instrument gets a spec plate — the states it ships, stamped provenance, where-used pins, path — because the question this page answers is "what do we have and what is it made of," not "what could this render." The states row IS the API surface; if a state isn't on the plate, it doesn't exist. Demo motion here is honest: the playground is the data feed.

The call — provenance is a first-class stamp

Three stamp classes: FORGED IN-HOUSE (steel — all 13 RIG instruments, deliberately no Radix), RADIX ▸ * (amber — vendored behavior we restyled), CVA · NO RADIX (violet — styling machinery only). Dead stock wears the red band until it's wired in or deleted — the audit found three (dialog, tooltip, sonner), and this page makes that state impossible to forget. Registry-equivalent verdicts from the audit print on the plate (e.g. "badge — REJECTED, restyle not simplification").

The call — diagram over inspector, 70/30

The harness IS the answer — most "why" questions are really "what does this touch."
