Final synthesis · candidate lock

The room has a workbench.

Audio owns the floor; the workbench holds whatever the room is talking about. A stable cast rail shows who is speaking, working, waiting, or blocked. In the middle, one shared object—screen glance, sketch, file, decision—lets agents and developer point at the same thing. Underneath, one room tape makes every solo exchange and group call replayable without turning the product into six chat tabs.

08

The decisions behind it

This is a synthesis with cuts, not a museum of every idea. The product’s center is shared attention; operational completeness stays one layer away.

BET 01

Objects, not bolt-ons

Glances, drawings, files, decisions, and pasted images all enter conversation through the workbench. Audio explains the object; the tape keeps them synchronized.

BET 02

One room, filtered views

A room tape enables real group context. Selecting Donnie filters the tape and targets the composer; it does not teleport into a separate chat universe.

BET 03

Stable people, dynamic attention

The cast rail never reorders under the pointer. Hot seats expand in place. This keeps character presence without equal-card indifference or state-sorted pointer roulette.

BET 04

Speech is white light

Achromatic on-air signal cannot collide with persona colors. Persona hue marks identity and authorship; semantic state uses lamp, word, posture, and sound.

BET 05

Audio etiquette is product design

Working is silent. Waiting knocks once. Blocked repeats sparingly. PTT ducks the current voice and yields at a clause boundary. A lovable room is one you do not mute.

BET 06

Attention defines surfaces

Room = work. Shoulder = glance. Pocket = remote. They share floor, cast, object, and tape—but reveal only what each distance can support.
