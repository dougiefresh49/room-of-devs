Synthesis · The Air Desk

Who’s talking — and what did they just say.

Thesis. The Room is a broadcast desk, not a dashboard of equal tiles. Hierarchy is driven by signal state (on air → asking → hand up → working → idle → stale), rendered as channel strips so the speaking agent can grow words without stealing space from everyone else. ON AIR wins by light, not hue — an achromatic white treatment that can never collide with Raph’s red or Donnie’s purple — while persona color stays on the talent (avatar, spine, grant ring). The interpreter doesn’t shrug; it prints a receipt. Playful in copy, instrument-precise in chrome.

Geometry & motion

4px base. Radius is equipment-grade: 5 controls, 8 strips, 12 panes, 20 sheets, 999 only for lamps and the round talkback key.

Motion budget: lamps breathe, on-air meter animates, PTT ring pulses. Avatar’s 70ms frame swap is the only fast thing — ring/glow/lamp live on siblings so img.src never touches React.

Sans for speech. Mono for telemetry (state, timers, model, path, spend). That split alone tells you what’s voice and what’s console.

08 Rationale

Contested calls, settled

This is a synthesis with taste — not a merge. Each call below names the winner and why the losers lost.

ON AIR is light, not hue

Boards split: amber (B), teal/green (C/D), white (A). White wins. With four saturated persona accents, any hue for “speaking” eventually collides — Raph-red vs coral-hand, Mikey-orange vs amber-speak, Donnie-purple vs violet-ask. Achromatic luminance is the only treatment that works identically across the cast. Hue is reserved for working (cyan), hand (amber), asking (pink) — chosen to stay off the jersey palette.

Strips over equal tiles

B/D’s tile walls look like a “room of people.” A’s strips read like an instrument. Strips win on desktop because the speaking agent is the only one with content worth reading — it needs a caption line, talkback bar, and transport without reflowing the wall. Sort order is state-driven; the thing you need is always near the top. Phone keeps compact cards (thumb scroll).

Broadcast metaphor: medium

C’s “THE ROOM IS ON AIR” is too loud for a daily tool. Enterprise-quiet is too dead. Dial to booth language: ON AIR, hold the room, cue, talkback, program pane, lease — in mono labels and chrome, not in every sentence of body copy. Characters still sound like themselves in spoken summaries.

Interpreter = auditable receipt

Consensus across B/C/D: plans are thread objects, not vanishing toasts. Take A’s destructive gating + spend breadcrumbs, D’s auto-resume step, C’s “Room remains held” footer. Heard utterance at top, outcome under every step, Undo on the plan. Reasoning private; commands public.

PTT: persona grant, white speak

B wanted white PTT (mic event ≠ persona). D painted the grant in persona color (who hears you). Both, for different moments: grant ring = persona (identity of target); on-air treatment = white (room signal). Talkback is always the widest target on its container.

Confirm is a spoken sentence

Universal winner across B/C/D (A agrees in spirit). “Start Donnie · sonnet · jellyfin-streamline” is the receipt and the CTA — same pattern as the interpreter repeating what it heard. D’s “Send him in →” stays; it’s the right amount of personality.

Cut vs the feature list

  • Per-card transport everywhere. Only speaking / hand-up show full transport; idle/working use ⋯.
  • Swap persona on the card face. Rare + dangerous mid-call — DropdownMenu with confirm.
  • Status-info as a permanent button. Hover / long-press card detail instead.
  • “Replay slower” as a peer control on phone. Speed ToggleGroup owns it; keep ½ only on desktop/dock.
  • Grid density / card size preferences. Strips are one size; density comes from state sort.
  • Hide-agent as a top-level phone action. Overflow — chat deserves the thumb slot.

Add vs the feature list

  • Playback lease chip — which device owns audio, always visible.
  • Cue count + catch-up — play unheard oldest-first at 1.25×.
  • Interpreter history — every plan kept; trust requires an audit trail.
  • Destructive-step gating in plans — stop and ask before kill/spawn.
  • Spend breadcrumbs — this app bills per sentence; hide that and you train bad habits.
  • Frozen top slot while on air — speaking strip doesn’t jump out from under the cursor mid-click.
