# Design brief — "Room of Devs" (final synthesis pass)

This is the last concept round before a real mock gets built. You have
the full history of the design exploration in this directory, and your
job is to produce THE concept that best pulls it all together.

Read everything here — and nothing else. Do not look at any source code
or files outside this directory; no network calls.

## What's in this directory

Three prior rounds, all for the same product. Boards are anonymized —
different designers, identified only by letters (letters are NOT
consistent across groups):

1. **The detailed round** — `brief-detailed.md` is the original
   full-feature brief. `synth-A/B/C/D.html` are four "best of
   everything" candidates, each synthesized from an earlier set of
   detailed concepts. (These boards may internally reference concept
   letters from their own earlier round — ignore those references.)
2. **Open concept round 1** — `brief-wild1.md` is a deliberately vague
   brief; `round1-A/B/C.html` are the owner's favorite boards from it.
   These produced the most novel material of the program.
3. **Open concept round 2** — `brief-wild2.md` re-ran the open pass
   seeded with the product's near-future goals; `round2-A/B/C.html`
   are the keepers.

## Your task

Design the definitive concept. Pick and choose the strongest elements
across all three rounds, or simply take inspiration from them — and
fuse them into one coherent direction that covers the full product
(the detailed brief's scope) AND integrates the near-future goals as
structural, first-class parts:

- **Screen awareness** — consentful "look at what I'm looking at".
- **Drawing / whiteboard** — agents sketch diagrams; the developer
  draws back. Both directions.
- **The group call** — several agents + the developer in ONE
  multi-voice conversation, not parallel monologues.
- **Typed replies + attachments everywhere** — type instead of speak
  on the Mac; every reply surface (Mac and phone) accepts a pasted
  image or file.

You are NOT limited to what has already been designed. Where you see a
better way, re-visualize the concept differently — the prior boards
are raw material and provocation, not a boundary. A genuinely better
idea beats a faithful merge. But be intentional: when you adopt,
adapt, or reject a major element from the prior rounds, your rationale
should make the choice legible (a short provenance note per major
surface is welcome).

## Constraints (unchanged)

- Audio is the primary channel; the UI is its companion.
- Character avatars are images that can swap frames for lip-sync.
- One small Mac surface must stay visible over other apps without ever
  stealing focus.
- Phone use is one-handed.
- Implementation will be React + Tailwind + shadcn/ui — but do NOT let
  that limit the concept.

## Deliverable

EXACTLY ONE fully self-contained HTML concept board written to the
output path you were given: inline CSS, no external requests, system
fonts, placeholder avatars (initials in colored circles — don't draw
turtles), real sample copy (never lorem ipsum), responsive with no
horizontal page scroll. Include: your thesis, your tokens (palette +
type), mockups of every surface you propose, provenance notes for
major choices, and a short rationale. Opinionated beats complete.
