Thesis

Persona says who. Tally says what now. The interface gives speech one unmistakable achromatic signal, preserves stable locations for everyone else, and uses the broadcast metaphor only where it improves orientation.

07 / RATIONALE

The calls, made deliberately.

The synthesis keeps the strongest common language across all four boards, resolves their contested choices, and narrows custom UI to the domain-specific interactions.

Achromatic “on air”

White is a luminance event, so it cannot be mistaken for a persona. It appears as one ring, one rail, and one filled label. Working cyan, hand amber, and asking pink are supporting tallies; every state also has text and form.

Stage + stable benches

An equal grid understates urgency; a fully sorted list moves too much. Priority states expand on the stage. Quiet identities retain seats. The stage freezes during pointer interaction, preventing mid-click reordering.

Broadcast, with restraint

“On air,” “cue,” “hold room,” and “casting” explain real behavior. Everything else speaks like developers do. The metaphor sets orientation; character voice supplies the fun.

Plans are receipts

The interpreter renders the heard phrase, public operations, outcomes, cost/cache breadcrumbs, and durable room state. It never presents hidden chain-of-thought. Destructive operations stop and confirm inline.

One talkback contract

Hold, immediate white enclosure, named destination, release to send, drag away to cancel. The dock gets a wide bar; phone gets thumb-zone placement. The room auto-holds while the mic is open.

Words get the space

Dock: one timed caption. Room: structured markdown. Phone: line focus plus a word sweep. All use the same audio cursor, while the lip-sync image swaps independently at ~70ms.

  • CUTFull transport on idle/working cards; transport follows audio, not identity.
  • CUTDecorative persona washes across panels; identity lives at the avatar and selection edge.
