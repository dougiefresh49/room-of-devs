# Synthesis brief — Room of Devs redesign, round 2 of 2

Four designers each produced an independent concept board for the
product described in `design-brief.md` (read it first — its feature
inventory, constraints, and deliverable rules all still apply, INCLUDING
the blind rule: do not open the repo's UI source, and make no network
calls).

Their boards are in this directory as `board-A.html`, `board-B.html`,
`board-C.html`, `board-D.html` (authorship anonymized; treat them as
four peers). Read all four carefully — including their token systems,
per-surface mockups, and rationale/mapping sections.

## Your task

Produce ONE new, complete concept board that synthesizes the strongest
version of this product from everything on the table:

- Take the best pieces from each board — a layout from one, a state
  system from another, a microcopy voice from a third.
- You may modify anything you borrow, resolve conflicts between boards
  your own way, and add ideas none of them had. This is a synthesis with
  taste, not a merge. If a board's approach to some surface is simply
  the best, adopt it and improve it; where all four are weak, do better.
- Decide the contested points deliberately. Examples you'll notice: what
  color/treatment "speaking / on air" gets and why it can't be confused
  with persona accents; equal-tile grid vs state-sorted layout; how loud
  the broadcast metaphor should be in the copy; how the interpreter's
  executed plan renders. Your rationale must state each call and why.

## Deliverable

Same rules as design-brief.md: EXACTLY ONE fully self-contained HTML
file (inline CSS, no external requests, system fonts, placeholder
avatars, real sample copy, phone-friendly responsive) with: (1) Thesis;
(2) Tokens; (3) all six surfaces mocked, showing at minimum one card in
every major state, the dock while speaking, the interpreter plan
rendering, and the picker confirm step; (4) Rationale — including the
shadcn/AI-Elements mapping AND a short provenance table: for each major
design decision, which board it came from (A/B/C/D, "modified", or
"new").

This synthesized board is a candidate for the final locked design
target. Make it the one.
