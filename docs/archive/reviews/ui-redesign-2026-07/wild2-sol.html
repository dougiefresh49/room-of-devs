Why this
holds together.

The concept makes a strong bet: the product is not four assistants with speech added. It is one ambient social space with four distinct collaborators.

  1. State becomes spatial. Character channels let a glance answer the only urgent questions: who’s talking, who’s working, and who needs me?
  2. Media becomes conversational. Screen, canvas, voice, text, and files are all message forms in one thread—available on every reply surface.
  1. The periphery stays respectful. The doorway carries sound and urgency across apps while the full studio owns depth and history.
  2. Play serves recall. Color, voice, and character make parallel work legible; the quiet utility shell keeps it credible all day.
