Room of Devs · redesign concept 03

The Night Studio
your agents, on air

This room is a late-night broadcast studio, not a dashboard. Speech is the headline event, so the whole visual system is built like studio signaling: semantic states are tally lamps (small, glowing, monospace-labeled — teal means ON AIR and outshines everything), while persona colors live only on the talent — avatar, ring, and name. Chrome stays warm charcoal and quiet; the characters and the one agent currently speaking carry all the light. Playful in copy, disciplined in color.

01 · TOKENS

Lamps for states, jerseys for personas

Two color systems that never trade jobs: state lamps are small + luminous + always paired with a monospace label; persona accents are large + matte + only ever on avatar/ring/name/spine-of-identity. A card can be Donnie-purple and ON-AIR-teal at once without confusion.

Key decisions

  • One glow per screen. The ON-AIR teal ring + waveform is the only luminous object anywhere. Working pulses gently; everything else is matte. A wall of 8 cards stays scannable because light = sound.
  • Lamps carry labels. Every state is lamp + monospace word, never color alone — readable across the room, colorblind-safe, and it gives the UI its console voice.
  • Personas are jerseys, not themes. Accent appears only on avatar, hold-ring, and the picker confirm. Cards never tint by persona, so state color always wins the glance.
  • The confirm is a sentence. “Start Donnie · sonnet · jellyfin-streamline” is the receipt and the button. Same pattern powers the interpreter receipt: the system repeats what it heard, then shows each step’s outcome.
  • Dock is read-mostly. Four targets, zero inputs, press-and-hold PTT — nothing can request keyboard focus, by construction.

Cut / added vs the feature list

  • CUTPer-card transport everywhere. Full transport shows only on speaking/hand-raised cards; idle/working collapse to ⋯ (DropdownMenu). Six buttons on eight cards is noise.
  • CUT“Swap persona” as a surface control. It’s rare — it lives in the ⋯ menu with kill (both destructive-ish, both confirmed).
  • CUTStatus-info dialogs. Folded into a hover/long-press card flip (uptime, model, cwd) instead of another modal.
  • ADDCatch-up as a first-class button (phone footer): “3 unheard” is the number that matters when you walk back in — it plays the queue in order, karaoke sheet open.
  • ADDInterpreter step “queued · auto” affordance — plans can include a deferred resume step, so “pause then replay” cleans up after itself.
  • ADDTicker dock state. Between speeches the dock earns its pixels as a one-line room status instead of hiding.
