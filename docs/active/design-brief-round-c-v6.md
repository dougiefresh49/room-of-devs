# Design Brief — Round C, v6 (THE RIG refinement — cohesion pass)

_2026-07-29. Delta on v4/v5. Owner reaction to the full play arc:
**"I like both but really like THE RIG the most."** BLACK // GLASS is
preserved (see Theme note below), and this round refines THE RIG's
desktop board into a cohesive rugged-industrial-tech language, guided
by real Titanfall reference images the owner supplied
(`concepts-round-c-v6/refs-titanfall/`)._

## Owner's refinement notes (binding, near-verbatim)

1. **Pull the design together.** The concept fragments sold "rugged
   industrial tech"; the board sometimes settles for generic panels.
   Named example: the v3/v4 spine fragment has **holo cards spawning
   off the metal spine** — physically docked, projected from it — while
   the RIG board renders "a grey bay and some cards next to it." The
   spine and its plan-cards must read as one machine, not a column plus
   a list.
2. **Titanfall corner language.** Their cards aren't rectangles: **1 or
   2 corners rounded or clipped differently** per card (see
   `faceplate-briggs-corner-clips.jpg` — big radius top-left, hard
   corners elsewhere, sub-panels echoing it; and
   `archer-scope-clipped-frame.jpg` — a scope frame with asymmetric
   chamfers). Doesn't have to match exactly — the point is a consistent
   asymmetric corner system that makes every card feel machined from
   the same shop.
3. **Hexagonal patterns.** Titanfall leans on hex textures
   (`harvester-hex-shell.jpg` — the Frontier Defense harvester, an
   energy machine wrapped in a glowing hex shell). Use hexes as a
   texture/motif layer (mesh fills, shield patterns, status shells),
   not as layout.
4. **The harvester itself** is offered as a concept — a glowing
   energy-core machine. Candidate uses: the concierge's physical
   presence, the spend/energy instrument, an idle "room power" object.
   Designer's judgment.
5. **Bonus ref:** `energy-tower-spine.jpg` — a vertical energy tower,
   hex-pattern translucent shields around a beam core on a machined
   base. The strongest single image of what THE SPINE could be.
   `map-callout-landmarks.jpg` shows Titanfall's map-key style (callout
   lines from a map to labeled thumbnails) — good language for legend /
   zoom-ladder annotations.

## Noted for LATER (do not build in v6)

- **The 10,000-ft view**: a sci-fi planetary/starmap canvas
  (`frontier-war-starmap.jpg` is the vibe) — the spine as origin,
  tickets/issues as **ships** with flight paths through **planets
  (phases)**, ending at a destination (naming TBD — needs a term as
  good as "the spine"). Owner: "kool concept, not necessary for day 1;
  note it and revisit once the other pieces are in place." Logged in
  the ideas backlog. A board may include a small "future: 10,000-ft
  view" annotation, nothing more.
- **Theme switching**: BLACK // GLASS (and the rest of the round-C
  boards) stay preserved in the repo so a theme system can resurrect
  them later. Not a day-1 feature.
