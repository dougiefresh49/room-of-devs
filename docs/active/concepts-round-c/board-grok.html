SIGNAL DESK

The room earns silence. The primary object is not a ticket and not a session — it is the short tray of things that broke silence, reconstructed every snapshot from spine operator-gate events, while work and spend sit as hinterland you open. Empty tray = the product working.

Beating the mortality argument (primary object ≠ work item)

A ticket is durable — but a full ticket catalog as primary UI recreates the same failure mode at a slower cadence: you stare at grinding inventory while the thing you actually needed (a plan-review, a blocker, a diagram) competes with noise. Sessions die at /clear; tickets proliferate into wallpaper. The tray dies and reforms with every gate event — keyed to attention debt, not a context window. Work items remain first-class in the hinterland and in ticket detail; they are just not what the window is for. Salience is why this round exists — so salience is the noun.

Surface doctrine

Three surfaces, three jobs. They do not share a shape.

  • Desk — tray + hinterland + policy + talk
  • Dock — lamp + one signal + PTT. Never a list.
  • Phone — away supervision: waiting-on-you only

Concierge presence

Mikey is a booth rail, not a stage star. Small avatar, vertical name, status lamp. Expands only into captions when speaking. Checkout voice swaps the booth face with a temporary badge — never a second card in a grid.

Anti-goal self-check

Closest call · anti-goal #1

Not a voice+KB chatbot. Elevating the tray and demoting the cast risks reading as “chat with Mikey plus a todo list.” The counter is structural: work state, spend, and salience policy are visible with the mic closed and the tray empty — the conversation is never the whole app. Still the nearest miss, because the booth + composer are persistent chrome. Watch that in the pick: if the tray ever becomes optional secondary, this board has failed #1.
