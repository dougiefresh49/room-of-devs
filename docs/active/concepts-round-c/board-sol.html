Signal Desk

A quiet editorial control desk where durable work stays visible, speech is a scarce broadcast channel, and the owner sees only the decisions worth interrupting life for.

The strong opinion

The primary object is the obligation: a durable work item plus the decision it may eventually require. Sessions are not shown. Workers are not shown. The screen asks only: what exists, what changed, and what is mine to decide?

This beats mortality because an obligation is keyed to the spine, survives every context window, and settles into artifacts and recorded decisions. Threads are machinery behind the ledger—not furniture in the room.

01 / DURABLE NOUN

Rows are tracker-backed obligations, never session cards. Context death changes no position or label.

02 / ATTENTION GRAMMAR

Orange means the owner owes a decision. Amber/blue agent-state semantics are retired from the primary view.

03 / SPEND IS EDITORIAL

Billed truth and forward estimate are visibly different. A frontier borrow is a line item, not a sparkle.

04 / NO PROCESS BOARD

No drag, lanes, or state transitions. This is a read-through of the spine with scoped commands.

Surface contract

Desktop, dock, and phone do not share a shape because they do not share a job.

  • Desktop: read the room and inspect evidence.
  • Dock: govern what may interrupt you.
  • Mobile: clear decisions without waking or stalling threads.
  • Every reply surface: voice, type, and attachment have parity.
  • All state: rendered from daemon snapshots; drafts and open panels are view state only.

07 / COST

Price what this invents

No new backend

Ledger projection

Read tracker/thread state already destined for the daemon snapshot; the UI merely groups it by owner obligation.

Small daemon + protocol

Salience policy snapshot

Expose per-event-class speak / lull / log policy plus shared queue counts and mutation commands.

Small accounting seam

Cost rollups

Aggregate the required per-call log into billed and estimated fields by work item; no database, rebuildable from files.

Small artifact metadata

Proof-sheet versions

Snapshot file path, render path, version label, kept status, and source work item; git/files remain the store.

No new backend

PTT surfaces

Reuse the existing single-owner PTT/grant path; the board changes affordance and copy, not capture ownership.

No new backend

Concierge lens

Existing avatar frames can be clipped into the lens and mutated by ref; no new render-driven stage or lipsync logic.

08 / CHECK

Anti-goal self-check

01
Not a voice + KB chatbot

The work ledger dominates; conversation is a scoped control and artifact-question loop.

02
No multi-voice theatre

One avatar/lens appears. Checkout replaces its identity and names an expiry.

03
No real-time ink

Artifacts are rendered versioned files, with no canvas or stroke data.

04
No lipsync escalation

The lens admits existing ref-mutated frames; its CSS ring is cosmetic only.

05
No always-listening mic

Every mic state says hold, release, or cancel. Rest explicitly says quiet.

06
No resident brain

Every answer cites ticket/artifact source and names the borrowed tier and cost.

07
No new orchestrator runtime

The ledger is a projection with no drag-to-transition or process authoring.

08
No client conversation state

Visible facts derive from snapshots; only selection, open panels, and drafts are local.

09
No room-state database

Costs and artifact indexes are rebuildable projections of logs, files, and tracker state.

10
No silent capability growth

Keep/file and saved-tool promotions use named, visible confirmation language.

Closest call: #7, “no new orchestrator runtime.” The obligation ledger can look like a bespoke task manager. The guardrail is structural: it has no lanes, drag operations, arbitrary status editing, or process design. Every row links back to and mirrors the tracker; commands are limited to the explicit decision currently requested.
