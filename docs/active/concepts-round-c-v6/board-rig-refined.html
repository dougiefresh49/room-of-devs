The spine is one machine: plans clamp to an energized rail — dock boss, projection stem, beam core, hex shielding; archived plates bolt on dark metal struts v4's grey bay with cards floating next to it The owner's named complaint dies; costs a fixed dock gutter, zero data change
One machined corner grammar — big radius top-left, chamfered cut bottom-right — echoed at three scales through shared CSS clips Default rounded rectangles, each component its own shape Every card reads as milled in one shop; outer glows migrate from box-shadow to drop-shadow filters

The harvester lands as the spend instrument — a hex-shelled energy core whose lit cells are the month's draw Harvester as Mikey's housing, or an idle room-power prop The energy metaphor sits on the thing that actually burns money; Mikey keeps his faceplate

Needs real new engineering: the salience quantity + threshold (the #75 roll-up work) — now feeding TWO instruments (the ring and every plot position); the watch-order subscription verb; per-thread token/cost roll-ups in PanelSnapshot; STT→option-id resolution for voice-answered questions; and the saved-verb registry files. The reply paths themselves — tmux inject, PTT + grant, the AskUserQuestion hold — are daemon machinery that already exists. The v6 cohesion pass adds zero new engineering — docks, corner cuts, hex shields and the harvester are CSS over the same snapshot.

⚠ WHAT I'D CUT TO REIN IT IN CHEAP VERSION NAMED FOR EACH
CUT 1 · LIVE PLOT DRIFT + TRAILS

Continuously animated craft positions need per-thread salience streaming and motion interpolation the daemon doesn't compute today.

CHEAP: recompute positions on each snapshot rev and CSS-transition between them; drift trails and vector arrows cut.
CUT 2 · MULTI-ROOM ON THE PLOT

The podlink structure at range implies merging snapshots across rooms — a second daemon surface for a room that doesn't exist yet.

CHEAP: one room v1; other rooms are a dim badge with a count in the plot corner. The manifest file is still the seam.
CUT 3 · ZOOM AS REAL NAVIGATION

Continuous pinch/scroll morphing plot → rail → node is a bespoke camera system.

CHEAP: three fixed views with hard cuts (buttons / double-click a blip). The ladder is the contract, not the tween.
CUT 4 · FREE-SPEECH OPTION MATCHING

Resolving “yeah do the shared one” to option 1 needs an LLM hop per answer plus a clarify loop.

CHEAP: exact grammar only — “option one/two/three” and the option's own two-word label, in the RuleRouter. Zero LLM.
CUT 5 · HARVESTER CELL THEATER

Per-cell shell lighting that reacts to live burn — hexes igniting as calls land — needs per-call events streamed to the panel and a DOM node per hex.

CHEAP: what's drawn here — one hex texture, a conic mask at the month fraction, one core pulse. Snapshot spend totals only.
CUT 6 · LIVE TAILS EVERYWHERE + DOCKING CINEMATICS

Streaming a tail into every expanded node, and cards physically sliding onto the rail with a beam surge on spawn/settle, are theater the snapshot can't pay for.

CHEAP: tail only the open node; docks are static — the stem's conduit flow is the only motion. Threshold stays a config.json number.
